Privacy policy

GLADSTATS is built to work without knowing who you are. You can use every core feature without an account, and your milestones stay on your device unless you choose to back them up.

What is stored on your device

Your milestones — the title, emoji, category, date and whether you are counting up or down — are saved locally with your app settings. Nothing leaves your device unless you sign in.

If you sign in

Signing in with Apple or Google is optional. It exists so your milestones can be backed up and restored on another device. We then store your milestones and your account identifier with Google Firebase, and only your own account can read them. Sign out at any time to stop syncing.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by the App Store and by RevenueCat, which tells the app whether your Pro access is active. We never see or store your payment details.

Notifications

Your daily reminder is scheduled on your device. The text is generated locally from your own milestones and is not sent through any server.

Share cards

Share images are created on your device. They go only where you send them — nothing is uploaded automatically.

Deleting your data

You can delete any milestone in the app, or remove them all from Settings. Deleting them while signed in also removes them from the backup. Deleting the app removes everything stored locally.

Children

GLADSTATS is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Contact

Questions about privacy are welcome at info@gladstats.com.
