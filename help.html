Help & FAQ

Everything you need to get the most out of GLADSTATS. If your question is not answered here, get in touch — we read every message.

Adding a milestone

Tap the add button, then pick a preset or write your own title. Choose whether you are counting up from a past date or down to a future one, set the date and time, and save. Touch and hold a milestone on the home screen to edit or delete it.

How the daily stat is chosen

Every day GLADSTATS looks across all your milestones and every unit of time — hours, days, weeks and months — for the most remarkable number. Round numbers like 500 or 100,000 and repeating patterns like 111,111 rank highest. When nothing special lands, it shows the most meaningful plain count instead, so there is always something to see.

Daily reminder

Turn on Glad Stat in Settings to receive up to two notifications at 09:00 and 21:00 when a meaningful pattern appears. On quiet days, you may receive an approaching milestone at 09:00. If nothing arrives, check that notifications are allowed for GLADSTATS in your device settings.

Widgets

With Pro you can add a GLADSTATS widget to your home or lock screen. Touch and hold your home screen, tap the add button, search for GLADSTATS and choose a size. The widget refreshes shortly after midnight, so the number is right even if you have not opened the app.

Archiving a milestone

Touch and hold a milestone on the home screen and choose Archive. It disappears from your home screen and daily reminder but keeps its full history, and it no longer counts towards the free limit. Restore or permanently delete archived milestones from Settings.

What Pro includes

The free version tracks up to three milestones. Pro removes that limit and adds home and lock-screen widgets plus advanced micro-stats.

Restoring a purchase

Signed in with the same Apple ID and Pro is missing — for example on a new phone? Open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchases.

Backup and moving devices

GLADSTATS works fully offline and keeps your milestones on your device. Sign in from Settings to back them up, then sign in with the same account on a new device to bring them across.

Still stuck?

Email info@gladstats.com and tell us what happened. Mentioning your device and the app version from Settings helps us a lot.
